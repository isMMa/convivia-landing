Frequently asked questions

Is Convivia free?+

Yes, Convivia is free to download on Google Play.

What kind of rentals is Convivia for?+

It's built for landlords who rent out rooms or shared flats and want to manage contracts, payments and tenants from their phone instead of spreadsheets or WhatsApp groups.

Can I manage my tenants' payments?+

Yes. Convivia tracks each tenant's rent payments and sends automatic reminders as a due date approaches.

Is Convivia available on iPhone?+

Yes, Convivia is available on both Google Play and the App Store.

How does Convivia protect my data?+

Data is encrypted in transit and you can request deletion at any time. Full details are in the privacy policy.
